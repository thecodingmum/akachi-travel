# Brag Plan: Akachi Travels

## Rubric (Step 1)

1. **What is the app?** A luxury travel agency site: Akachi Travels plans, books and handles bespoke journeys (flights, visas, hotels, packages, personalised itineraries) and converts visitors through a "Let's draw your map" trip-brief form.
2. **Most impressive claim:** "Journeys, composed by hand." / "4,800+ journeys composed" / "72h to your first itinerary".
3. **Visual hook:** Black and gold. Giant Anton wordmark "AKACHI TRAVELS", gold `#c9a55c` on black `#0a0a0a`, cinematic Higgsfield destination footage.
4. **Real UI to show:** The trip-brief form at phone size: destination chips and "How can we help?" chips switching to gold, then the success state "YOUR MAP IS BEING DRAWN."
5. **Shortest satisfying video:** 20 s (user-specified).
6. **Tone:** preset `cinematic`; direction: *vertical luxury travel-agency launch reel, kinetic one-word typography over drone footage, modelled on the reference Short (PANAMA TRAVEL CO.)*.
7. **Audio:** Afrobeats (user request): HeyGen music library track "upbeat modern afrobeats background music with a positive energetic rhythm" (Astral c3c5baa8, 32 s, 120 BPM, very stable grid). Cuts on the beat, sparse impacts on reveals, soft taps on UI chips, one bell on the logo.
8. **Share caption:** "Akachi Travels. Journeys, composed by hand. 12 destinations, flights, visas and packages, your first itinerary in 72 hours."
9. **User flow:** Pick destinations on the brief form → pick services → brief received: "Your map is being drawn."

## What is this app?
A black-and-gold luxury travel agency that composes bespoke journeys by hand, from Santorini to Singapore, and gets you a first itinerary in 72 hours.

## The angle
A 20-second vertical travel-ad in the grammar of the reference Short (word-at-a-time display type over fast drone cuts, a fill-to-outline title card, stat cards with solid label boxes, an iris ring, a squeeze-to-line into a boxed end card) but rebuilt in Akachi's own identity: black and gold, Anton slabs, a serif-italic accent word, and the site's real form flow as the proof. It should feel like a brand film that happens to end with the booking working.

## Hook (first 2.5 s)
Frame 0 is already a picture: Santorini's caldera pool at sunset with "THE" in heavy black Anton on the gold sky. Then "WORLD" · "IS" · "WAITING." slam in one per beat. Grounded: the site's closing line "The world is waiting."

## Key moments
- **Iris ring → title card:** a thin gold ring opens on the beat into Singapore at blue hour; "AKACHI / TRAVELS" lands solid gold, then flips to outline-only on the next beat; a gold-ruled tagline bar draws open: "JOURNEYS, *composed* BY HAND."
- **Beat montage:** eight destinations cut every beat (0.5 s) with zoom-cuts, while thin, wide-tracked words hold over them: WANDER · UNWIND · INDULGE; a small gold coordinates ticker names each place.
- **Proof cards:** "4,800+" counts up over Dubai with a solid gold box "JOURNEYS COMPOSED"; "72H" over a Maldives sandbar with "TO YOUR FIRST ITINERARY"; then FLIGHTS / CRUISES / VISAS / PACKAGES stack in gold boxes over Amalfi.
- **The product working:** the last gold box floods the frame and becomes the real brief form; Santorini, Maldives, Dubai, Flight booking and Vacation package chips are tapped gold one per half-beat; whip to "YOUR MAP IS BEING DRAWN."

## Outro / punchline
The success screen squeezes into a single gold line (the reference's letterbox squeeze), which draws the logo box: star mark + AKACHI *Travels*, tagline tracks in, a gold "PLAN MY TRIP →" box types itself open, contact rows slide in. Hold.

## User flow worth showing

## Audio direction
- Role: cinematic support with a rhythmic bed
- Music: HeyGen library, `assets/music/afrobeats-heygen-c3c5baa8.mp3` ("upbeat modern afrobeats", 32 s), played from 0 s (its opening hit lands on frame 0); 0.2 s fade-in, 1.2 s fade-out ending on the 20.0 s downbeat
- Music treatment: volume ~0.6 (file true-peaks at +0.8 dBTP, so it is attenuated for headroom); cuts and word slams on the beat grid
- Music cue guidance: measured with `hyperframes audio` (signal provider): 120 BPM, beat interval SD 13 ms; beats at 0.52, 1.01, 1.51 … (every 0.5 s); downbeats every 2.0 s (2.02, 4.01, 6.01 …). Major locks: title at 2.0 s downbeat region (2.5 s beat), first stat at 9.0 s, flood into the product at 13.5 s, logo at 18.0 s downbeat. Montage cuts every beat 5.0–9.0 s; chip taps on beats/half-beats 14.0–15.5 s.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; music RMS warms the end-card ground (a faint gold radial on black). No waveform visuals.
- SFX posture: sparse-to-moderate, motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: hook word slams (soft impacts, first and last only), iris ring (deep bell-ish hit), stat count-up (chips stacking), chip taps (clicks), CTA typing (keypresses), logo (bell).
- Restraint rule: no SFX on every montage cut; never stack more than two cues on one beat.

### Scene 2 — Title — 2.5–5.0 s (2.5 s)
Gold ring opens from centre into Singapore at blue hour. "AKACHI" / "TRAVELS" lands solid gold (2.5), flips to outline on 3.5; tagline bar draws open at 3.0: "JOURNEYS, *composed* BY HAND." (serif-italic gold accent word). Small mono label above: "BESPOKE TRAVEL ATELIER".

### Scene 3 — Montage — 5.0–9.0 s (4 s)
